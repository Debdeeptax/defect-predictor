What happens

  1. Your code is unpacked into an isolated workspace (it is parsed, never executed).
  2. Static metrics are extracted per function and class: McCabe complexity, Halstead, LOC, and CK OO metrics (Python).
  3. The active models estimate a defect probability for each unit; a file's risk is that of its riskiest unit.
  4. The workspace is deleted; only the metrics and predictions are kept in the report.
